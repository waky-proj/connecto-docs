Purpose & scope

A useful connection

Members publish Needs and Offerings, discover suitable help, agree explicit terms and coordinate. The expanded MVP records each participant’s outcome and feedback. A member can act as requester or provider.

Two delivery horizons

Foundation POC · S0–S3: identity, durable profiles/listings, valid terms and useful explainable discovery.

Expanded MVP · S4–S11: safety, requests, agreements, persistent coordination, independent completion, feedback, optional photos and pilot measurement.

Scope boundaries

Payments, escrow, logistics, subscriptions, complex bidding, professional certification and composite trust scores are outside scope. Ratings and identity checks do not guarantee safety, competence or payment protection.

Requirements baseline: Business Requirements v2.0 / DCR-008, published on docs/consolidated-dcr-008-v2 at 83f0f127. The local change register records later approved changes and implementation status through DCR-022; current canonical files are Business Requirements v2.1 and Architecture, Plan and Technology v2.14. This page does not revise scope or approve unresolved policies. Consult the register for publication status.

Initial stack: React/JavaScript → ASP.NET Core → SQL Server, with Cognito identity. Sixteen repositories exist; app/API contain implementation, while BFF/domain/platform scaffolds represent future extraction, subject to operational gates.

Decisions and alternate paths

Listing & discovery
Validate terms → publish → exclude ineligible candidates → rank → show reasons. Invalid input returns for correction; no candidate shows an empty state. Owner edit/pause/close and stale-revision handling have local evidence, with release acceptance open.
Connection & agreement
Recipient declines or sender withdraws → recorded end state. Acceptance rechecks eligibility/capacity and records terms atomically. Same-key retries return the same result; conflicting revisions fail. Capacity policy needs explicit approval.
Outcome confirmation · S8 local
No reports → awaiting confirmation.
One report → one reported.
Both completed reports → both confirmed.
Incomplete or contested outcome → dispute.
A timeout never supplies the other participant’s confirmation.
Feedback & media · S9–S10 local
Role-specific reviews open after both-confirmed completion; reciprocal publication follows each chosen delay. Optional photos use private quarantine, format checks and parent permissions. Reporting stays available for incomplete, cancelled and disputed work.
